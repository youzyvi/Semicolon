About Semicoloned

At Semicoloned, we believe that everyone should have the opportunity to learn how to code, regardless of their experience or background.

We are a student-led initiative that provides free coding workshops for students, making programming more accessible to those who are curious about technology and eager to learn. Whether you're writing your first line of code or exploring more advanced concepts, we're here to help you take the next step.

Everyone starts somewhere. That's why our courses cater to a wide range of skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced programmers looking to deepen their understanding. We work with students to identify their learning needs and provide courses suited to their interests and abilities. Our goal is to make learning coding approachable, engaging and rewarding.

Our Mission

We aim to break down barriers to coding education by creating opportunities for students to learn, experiment and grow without the cost of traditional courses.

At Semicoloned, we hope to build a community where knowledge is shared, curiosity is encouraged and more students are empowered to explore the world of technology.
